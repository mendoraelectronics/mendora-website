THE OPPORTUNITY

Repair businesses need parts. Sourcing them is still fragmented.

Technicians often source parts through calls, local dealers and scattered WhatsApp groups. Finding the right board or component, comparing offers and confirming condition can take repeated follow-ups — and model-specific or uncommon parts are harder still.

  1. 01
    Scattered supplier discoveryParts are found across calls, local dealers and messaging groups rather than one place.
  2. 02
    Model-specific compatibilityThe right board depends on exact part numbers and device models.
  3. 03
    Unclear availabilityIt is often hard to know who has a part until several suppliers have been asked.
  4. 04
    Repeated follow-upsComparing offers and tracking an order can take many back-and-forth messages.
  5. 05
    Uncertain conditionConfirming that a part works, and what a quotation includes, is not always straightforward.

OUR MODEL

One platform. Three sourcing routes.

Not every part is bought the same way. A common capacitor can be listed at a fixed price; a model-specific board usually needs a supplier to confirm what they have; a rare part may need someone to go and find it. Mendora matches the route to the need.
